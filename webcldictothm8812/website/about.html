About LUNETT ESCAPES

A Colombo-born agency that turns travel wishes into smooth, well-priced journeys.

Our story

2011: Founded by Mishayel Silva and Tharoosh Perera in a two-desk office in Colombo.

2015: Opened our Kandy branch and launched group tours to Southeast Asia.

2019: Passed 10,000 happy travellers.

2024: Launched honeymoon and luxury collections.

Vision, mission and values

Vision

To be South Asia's most trusted travel partner.

Mission

Deliver safe, affordable, personal travel with honest advice.

Core values

Integrity, safety, curiosity, and respect for local communities.
